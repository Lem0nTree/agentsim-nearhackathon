# AgentSIM pitch

AgentSIM is building phone-based control for personal NEAR AI agents, with the owner's signing key protected by a compatible, provisioned SIM or eSIM. This proposed phone experience would let you approve spending without carrying another device. The cloud model proposes a payment, your SIM signs the permission you choose, and the NEAR vault enforces those limits.

Imagine asking your agent to buy a small amount of compute while you get on with your day. You choose the recipient, per-payment limit, total budget and expiry, and can revoke permission. A separate agent session key signs each request, and a restricted relayer submits it to the vault.

We have a concrete local proof. The actual product applet signed an owner grant and revocation in a PC Java Card simulator. A separate Ed25519 agent signed the spend, and a restricted Ed25519 relayer submitted it to a local NEAR sandbox. The receiver gained 0.001 sandbox NEAR and the transfer callback succeeded. The node rejected four actions outside the relay key's permissions; the vault rejected overspending and a payment after revocation. The proposal was a fixture. A live NEAR AI request returned HTTP 402 without a completion. The tested physical card lacked the native ECC required by the applet. No physical product signature or public-chain settlement has been demonstrated.
